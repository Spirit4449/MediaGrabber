July 3rd, 2026

Uploaded on 7/6/2026

From April 17th-19th, 2026, Mahant Swami Maharaj's vicharan in Surat included daily spiritual activities and extensive inspections of the ongoing Akshardham construction. On April 17th, he performed puja and gave discourses emphasizing the importance of Ghar Sabha for family harmony, inspired by Pramukh Swami Maharaj's teachings, highlighting that problems in homes are ancient and require spiritual solutions. The 18th was dedicated to a detailed inspection of the massive Akshardham complex, including the Mahant Swami Sabhagruh, welcome center, and various facilities, where he blessed countless workers and lauded their dedication to the challenging project, acknowledging it as a divine effort. On Akha Trij, April 19th, he performed the sacred puja of the golden kalashes destined for the Akshardham shikharas, making a solemn resolve for the mandir's lasting spiritual impact and for all visitors to achieve a 'Brahmic' state. Throughout these days, he deeply engaged with every detail, appreciating the scale and spiritual significance of the divine undertaking, referring to Akshardham as a "college of Brahm."

July 6th, 2026

Uploaded on 7/6/2026

In a series of discourses from June 12-21, 2026, delivered from Sarangpur, Mahant Swami Maharaj emphasized the importance of seeing Maharaj in everyone and developing `vivek` in daily life, especially with technology, to avoid agitation. Swami also highlighted the significance of documenting spiritual wisdom and `vicharan` details, praising such efforts as invaluable 'treasure.' He consistently advocated for cultivating `mahimā` (divine glory) in others while strictly avoiding `abhav-avgun` (fault-finding), stressing that `samp` (unity) is a personal endeavor. Emphasizing `niṣṭhā` in Bhagwan and the Satpurush, he encouraged adhering to `niyam-dharma`, diligently engaging in `satsang`, and maintaining physical well-being through regular exercise as essential for spiritual progress and attaining `brahmarūp` status.

July 5th, 2026

Uploaded on 7/6/2026

On April 20th and 21st, 2026, in Surat, Mahant Swami Maharaj inaugurated the Tapomurti Nilkanth Varni, performing its pujan and expressing profound joy, encouraging devotees to emulate Nilkanth Varni's tapas. He then extensively inspected the ongoing Akshardham project, including the water show and exhibition halls, providing clear guidance on presentation and emphasizing the spiritual essence of Akshardham. On April 21st, he performed puja in the sanctum, ceremonially installed a central pillar with his own hands, and then spent considerable time observing and blessing the dedicated artisans. He drew a design on an upcoming Shriji Maharaj murti, expressed deep affection for the workers, and offered insights on the supremacy of Guru's grace in spiritual endeavors. Despite physical discomfort from the heat and dust, he maintained a joyful demeanor, prioritizing the spiritual welfare and happiness of all involved.

July 4th, 2026

Uploaded on 7/6/2026

On April 19th and 20th, 2026, in Surat, Mahant Swami Maharaj presided over significant spiritual ceremonies for the upcoming Akshardham. On the first day, he performed the Kalash Pujan and Dhajadand Pujan atop the temple via a forklift, enduring physical strain at 93 years old, offering blessings for success and prosperity. The following day, he performed the pratishtha (consecration) of the 49-foot Nilkanth Varni murti, introducing new, loving rituals like gently placing his hands on the murti's eyes and offering food directly, despite the height and morning sun. Throughout both days, Swamishri emphasized selfless seva, inner Akshardham, and the importance of responsibility, loyalty, and understanding, inspiring thousands through his tireless example. The events, marked by massive devotee participation, culminated with an auspicious sprinkle of rain, seen as 'amrut bindu' from a yagna.
