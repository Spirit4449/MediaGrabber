May 7th, 2026

Uploaded on 5/11/2026

On January 1-3, 2026, in Ahmedabad, Mahant Swami Maharaj began the year emphasizing "antar-drashti" for spiritual growth, focusing on seeing one's own faults and others' virtues. He successfully prayed for a rain-free yagna and reviewed temple projects, particularly admiring the sculpted divine incidents for the Paris Mandir, blessing the artisans. On the second day, he stressed the paramount importance of Katha-varta for internal purity and progress, and taught that "one who abandons their mind is an Ekantik" (singularly devoted), while also blessing political and religious leader Parindubhai Bhagat and inaugurating the grand "Vishwa Shanti Satsang Diksha Mahayagna." The third day, marking Gunatitanand Swami's 215th Diksha Day, saw Swami Maharaj keenly observe a subtle, overlooked detail in a historical image and deliver a discourse on becoming "Gunatit" (transcendent of the three Gunas) for true spiritual joy, concluding the yagna with blessings for all participants and appreciating a unique 2000-foot garland.

May 6th, 2026

Uploaded on 5/11/2026

Mahant Swami Maharaj's profound spiritual state, characterized by detachment from worldly pleasures and constant remembrance of God and Guru, is revealed through various incidents spanning from November 10, 1973, in Khajurdi, India, to January 6, 2025, in Nairobi, Kenya, and other global locations. In 1973, he dedicated early morning hours to write an 8-page letter of spiritual guidance, demonstrating immense compassion for a young devotee. Later, in 2015, he meticulously crafted a personalized appreciation card for a host family, showcasing his attention to detail and ability to see divinity in everyday events, calling their home Akshardham. Consistently, even when advised to rest, he expressed being immersed in "prāpti no ānand" (bliss of divine attainment) and "bhajan" through uninterrupted remembrance of Yogiji Maharaj and the mantra "Aksharam Aham Purushottam Dasosmi." He displayed immense physical endurance during extensive vicharans, maintaining an inner state where waking and sleeping made no difference, often rising early not just out of habit, but out of deep concern for devotees' ability to perform darshan and attend their work.

May 11th, 2026

Uploaded on 5/11/2026

From April 22-28, 2026, Mahant Swami Maharaj was in Surat, where he emphasized performing service with love and glory, stating that all actions are accounted for. He blessed two families for their engagement, highlighting the importance of unity and humility for true happiness, and performed *dīkṣā* for Yāgn Bhagat, stressing firm adherence to spiritual principles. Swami Maharaj conducted site visits for the Akshardham construction, commending volunteers' tireless efforts, including women undertaking challenging physical tasks, and linked service to *mokṣa*, asserting that Maharaj Himself serves through devotees. He extensively discoursed on *sahaj sampark* (spiritual outreach), citing numerous examples of devotees initiating *satsang* assemblies, inspiring *pūjā* acceptance, connecting communities, and transforming lives through dedication, while also reiterating the profound merit of bringing one soul onto God's path. Despite physical fatigue, Swami Maharaj maintained immense enthusiasm and inquired about all aspects of service, affirming that such dedicated service ultimately transforms one's inner being into Akshardham.

April 8th, 2026

Uploaded on 5/9/2026

On November 26-27, 2025, in Ahmedabad, Mahant Swami Maharaj engaged in profound spiritual discourse and compassionate service. He began with early darshan and puja, addressing questions like overcoming anger through 'prapti no vichar' (realization of spiritual attainment), and met with Jagdishbhai Vishwakarma, the BJP Gujarat President, blessing his service-oriented aspirations. A significant event was the resolution of a complex family dispute regarding an impending marriage, for which Swamishri personally wrote letters emphasizing mutual understanding and being the 'best husband and wife'. He also delivered discourses on the importance of perfecting 'Nitya Puja' with devotion and understanding, and highlighted 'Ekantik Bhakti' as the sole purpose of human birth. Swamishri also remembered the departed devotee Dungarbhai Patel, subtly indicated that even a minor 'vasana' can impede entry into 'Dham', and underscored the non-negotiable importance of attending 'Ravi Sabha' (Sunday assembly).
